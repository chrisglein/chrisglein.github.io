### Faith - George Michael (1987)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0OfOiFFvoNHH0Ph1rRZI8F?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Released in 1987, "Faith" was a massive hit that showcased George Michael's talent as a solo artist after his departure from Wham!. The song topped the charts worldwide, and its iconic music video featuring Michael's leather jacket, tight jeans, and guitar became a defining image of the late '80s. "Faith" is often credited with helping Michael break free from his boy band image and establish himself as a serious musician. The song remains a beloved classic and a staple of '80s pop culture.

### Need You Tonight - INXS (1987)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5eU8qMd0TpaLqTGDZJaLDs?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Need You Tonight" was the Australian rock band INXS's only single to reach number one on the Billboard Hot 100. The song's blend of rock, funk, and dance elements helped solidify the band's place in the global music scene and remains one of their most enduring hits. The accompanying music video, with its innovative use of animation and live action, also gained significant attention. "Need You Tonight" is often seen as a quintessential '80s track that captures the era's eclectic musical influences.

### Never Gonna Give You Up - Rick Astley (1987)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/4PTG3Z6ehGkBFwjybzWkR8?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Never Gonna Give You Up" became a global phenomenon, topping the charts in 25 countries. The song gained a second life in the 2000s with the "Rickrolling" internet meme, making it a timeless piece of pop culture history. Its catchy melody and Astley's distinctive deep voice have made it a perennial favorite at parties and events. The song's unexpected resurgence in the digital age highlights its enduring appeal.

### Kokomo - The Beach Boys (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5qHYXcVvc9xsFB2uH7GpMN?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Kokomo" marked a surprising comeback for The Beach Boys. The song topped the Billboard Hot 100, becoming the band's first number-one hit since 1966. Its tropical vibe and inclusion in the movie "Cocktail" helped introduce the band to a new generation of fans. "Kokomo" became synonymous with beach and vacation culture, and its success reaffirmed The Beach Boys' lasting influence on American music.

### Fast Car - Tracy Chapman (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/2M9ro2krNb7nr7HSprkEgo?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Tracy Chapman's "Fast Car" is a poignant folk-rock song that tells a story of yearning and escape. The song's powerful lyrics and Chapman's soulful performance earned it critical acclaim and commercial success, making it a staple of contemporary music. It received multiple Grammy nominations and remains one of Chapman's signature songs. "Fast Car" continues to resonate with listeners for its timeless narrative and emotional depth.

### Miss You Much - Janet Jackson (1989)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/4r5VaK6H1bq0cTGPMTN97Z?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Miss You Much" was a lead single from Janet Jackson's album "Rhythm Nation 1814." The song's innovative production, choreography, and socially conscious themes helped solidify Jackson's status as a pop icon and influenced the sound of pop and R&B music in the '90s. Its success was further bolstered by its high-energy music video, which showcased Jackson's impressive dance skills. "Miss You Much" remains a defining track in her illustrious career.

### Don't Be Cruel - Bobby Brown (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0v9kGNjkKdQUdDoBIuiph4?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Don't Be Cruel" was a groundbreaking hit for Bobby Brown, blending R&B, pop, and new jack swing. The song's success helped define the sound of late '80s and early '90s R&B and established Brown as a leading figure in the genre. Its infectious beat and Brown's charismatic performance made it a standout track. "Don't Be Cruel" also paved the way for the rise of new jack swing as a dominant musical style.

### Straight Up - Paula Abdul (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5xl5582IihbEZAnfj0xyso?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Paula Abdul's "Straight Up" was a major hit that showcased her talents as both a singer and a choreographer. The song's catchy hook and innovative music video helped propel Abdul to stardom and solidified her place in '80s pop music history. Its success highlighted Abdul's unique ability to blend pop with dance elements. "Straight Up" remains one of her most memorable and beloved hits.

### Girl You Know It's True - Milli Vanilli (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0C7dYeCLF6ukChtW64UNge?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Girl You Know It's True" was a chart-topping hit for Milli Vanilli. However, the group's success was later overshadowed by a lip-syncing scandal that revealed they hadn't sung on their own records. The controversy remains one of the biggest scandals in pop music history. Despite this, the song's catchy melody and production continue to be recognized as part of the late '80s music landscape.

### Pour Some Sugar On Me - Def Leppard (1987)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/1aZLIbKEdsyqxyD6iNcrbA?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Pour Some Sugar On Me" is one of Def Leppard's most iconic songs. Its infectious chorus and blend of glam metal and pop rock helped it become a major hit and an anthem of the '80s rock scene. The song played a significant role in the success of their album "Hysteria," which became one of the best-selling albums of all time. "Pour Some Sugar On Me" remains a staple of rock radio and concert setlists.

### Can't Fight This Feeling - REO Speedwagon (1984)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5WwqdeavrQrbeAMDxGawse?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Can't Fight This Feeling" is a power ballad that became one of REO Speedwagon's biggest hits. The song's heartfelt lyrics and soaring melody made it a favorite for romantic occasions and solidified the band's place in '80s rock history. It reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and became a defining song of the decade. "Can't Fight This Feeling" continues to be celebrated for its emotional resonance and classic rock appeal.

[Heaven - Warrant](https://open.spotify.com/track/2dEgwtRjYl6TJkIwjRwn6z?si=f5c7f5cf41da4589)
"Heaven," released in 1989, is a power ballad that became Warrant's most successful single. The song's emotional lyrics and melodic guitar work helped it reach the top of the charts and became a defining song of the glam metal era. Its success contributed to the popularity of power ballads in the late '80s. "Heaven" remains a beloved track for fans of the genre and a testament to Warrant's musical legacy.

[Every Rose Has Its Thorn - Poison](https://open.spotify.com/track/5qaSekPPKHIEA1Yn0qkCaC?si=0a06d8ee8b7d41a3)
"Every Rose Has Its Thorn," released in 1988, is one of Poison's most famous songs. The power ballad's introspective lyrics and acoustic guitar made it a huge hit and an enduring anthem of the '80s rock ballad genre. It became the band's only number-one single on the Billboard Hot 100. "Every Rose Has Its Thorn" remains a quintessential '80s ballad and a symbol of the era's music.

### Cradle of Love - Billy Idol (1990)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/04fTyMhjCf9Xv6d2Sd5fKK?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Billy Idol's "Cradle of Love" was a major hit that showcased his signature blend of punk rock and pop. The song's success was bolstered by its memorable music video and helped maintain Idol's popularity into the '90s. It was featured in the soundtrack of the movie "The Adventures of Ford Fairlane," further increasing its exposure. "Cradle of Love" remains one of Idol's most recognizable and enduring songs.

### Wild Thing - Tone-Loc (1988)
<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0gljI0CtjpdZK6ecidfxto?utm_source=generator&theme=0" width="100%" height="352" frameBorder="0" allowfullscreen="" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

"Wild Thing" was a breakthrough hit for Tone-Loc and one of the first rap songs to achieve mainstream success. The song's catchy beat and humorous lyrics helped pave the way for the integration of hip-hop into popular music. Its success marked a significant moment in the acceptance of rap music in the mainstream. "Wild Thing" remains a classic example of early hip-hop's crossover appeal.
