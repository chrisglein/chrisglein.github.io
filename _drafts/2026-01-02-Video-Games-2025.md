This year, my gaming time clustered around a few very specific moments: evenings winding down, weekends with my daughter, and stretches where I wanted something beautiful, thoughtful, or quietly absorbing rather than loud or endless. A handful of games rose above the rest — not necessarily because they were the biggest or flashiest, but because they *stuck*.

Here are the ones that defined my year.
<!--more-->

## Hollow Knight: Silksong
[![Image](/media/posts/hollow-knight-silksong.jpg)](https://hollowknightsilksong.com/)
Silksong was the easiest lock on this list. It is absolutely worthy of the wait and a true successor to Hollow Knight — faster, sharper, and more confident in every way. The movement feels incredible, the world is dense with secrets, and every new area has that same *oh no, I’m going to be here for a while* energy that made the original so special. It doesn’t just meet expectations; it justifies them.

## Grounded
[![Grounded](/media/posts/grounded.jpg)](https://grounded.obsidian.net/)
Grounded completely surprised me. What I expected to be a novelty survival game turned into one of the most meaningful gaming experiences I had this year — largely because I played it deeply with my daughter. Base-building, spider-induced panic, gear experimentation, and slow mastery of a hostile backyard made this feel like a shared adventure rather than just another co-op game. Way better than I thought it would be, and way more memorable than most.

<p class="playLine"><span class='playIcon xbox'></span>Played on <a href="https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/grounded">Xbox GamePass</a></p>

## Blue Prince
[![Image](/media/posts/blue-prince.jpg)](https://www.blueprincegame.com/)
Blue Prince is a wonderfully mind-bending puzzle game that rewards curiosity, patience, and lateral thinking. Its shifting mansion layout and quiet tension made every session feel deliberate and thoughtful. This was another one I played in tandem with my daughter, comparing notes and theories, and that shared discovery made each small breakthrough feel bigger than it otherwise would have.

<p class="playLine"><span class='playIcon xbox'></span>Played on <a href="https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/blue-prince/9pfrtprq569q">Xbox GamePass</a></p>

## Indiana Jones and the Great Circle
[![Image](/media/posts/indiana-jones-and-the-great-circle.jpg)](https://indianajones.bethesda.net/en-US)
This is exactly the Indiana Jones game I wanted. It nails the tone — clever puzzles, cinematic set pieces, and globe-trotting momentum — without leaning too hard on nostalgia. It’s confident, playful, and consistently fun, the kind of game that reminds you how effective strong pacing and classic adventure design can still be.

<p class="playLine"><span class='playIcon xbox'></span>Played on <a href="https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/indiana-jones-and-the-great-circle">Xbox GamePass</a></p>

## Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
[![Image](/media/posts/clair-obscur-expedition-33.jpg)](https://www.expedition33.com/)
Clair Obscure: Expedition 33 is haunting in the best way. Its painterly visuals, surreal tone, and quiet sense of melancholy pulled me in immediately. It’s the kind of game that feels ambitious without being overwhelming — more interested in mood and world than constant action — and it lingered with me long after I stopped playing.

## And the Rest

These are games I spent time with and enjoyed, but not deeply enough *this year* to give them a full write-up.

* [Animal Well](https://www.animalwell.net/) — A beautifully strange puzzlebox of a world; I barely scratched the surface, but loved the atmosphere.
* [Wildfrost](https://www.wildfrostgame.com/) — Stylish, clever, and full of personality, even if I didn’t go all the way down the rabbit hole.
* [Celeste](https://www.celestegame.com/) — I started it and immediately understood the love; this one deserves a deeper dive once I finish it.
* [Keeper](https://www.doublefine.com/games/keeper) — Cozy and promising, but still early for me.
* [Winter Burrow](https://noodlecake.com/games/winter-burrow/) — Just getting started, but already radiating winter comfort.
