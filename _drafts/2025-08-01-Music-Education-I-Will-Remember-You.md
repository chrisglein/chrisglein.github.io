### Nothing Compares 2 U - Sinead O’Connor
- Originally a Prince song (recorded 1984)
- Had a terribly creepy interaction with Prince
- Shaved head after studio/record folks were pressuring her to have longer hair and be more feminine
- Understated music video with mainly just her head
- She quickly got sick of her #1 song
- Tore up Pope’s picture in 1992 as guest on SNL, protesting child abuse in Catholic Church
- Punk attitude
