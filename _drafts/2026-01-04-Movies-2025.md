In 2025 my movie watching split into two modes: the “let’s go see a *movie*” mode, and the “it’s a weeknight, we have snacks, let’s watch something with Neva” mode. I love both. One is for capital-E **Experience**, and the other is for the slow accumulation of shared references and inside jokes.

As usual, below are the movies I watched in 2025 that warranted commentary — ranging from *full gush* to *quick drive-by mention*.
