### Hunger Strike - Temple of the Dog (1991)
- Tribute to Mother Love Bone
- Chloe Crown of Thorns
- Eddie Vedder before Pearl Jam
- Also Cornell and Grohl
- No second verse so just change singer
- Broody and problematic dark grunge vibes

### Only Wanna Be With You - Hootie & The Blowfish (1995)
- Alternative to grunge, positive and jangly
- Darius Rucker is not Hootie
- 10th best selling US album ever. Top of 1995. 20 million.
- Southern rock sports bar band.

### Nuthin' But a 'G' Thang - Dr. Dre (1992)
- *The Chronic*
- Snoop Doggy Dogg
- NWA gangsta rap: Ice Cube, Eazy-E, Dr. Dre, more; *Boyz-n-the-Hood* produced by Dre
- NWA hit *F*** the Police*
- *Straight Outta Compton*
- NWA breaks apart for diverging interests
- Many allegations of violence against women and more, look up details
- Joins Death Row Records, co-founded by Suge Knight, former bodyguard for Bobby Brown
- Moog synthesizer
- "Bow wow wow yippie yo yippie yay"
- March 3, 1991 Rodney King police brutality followed by acquittals, then riots
- G-funk, rap music for subwoofers and low riders
- Dre doesn’t like his voice, wants to be recording not recorded
- Not a crossover hit really, established rap on its own
- Dre brought Tupac and Eminem to fame
- Introduced suburban households to gangsta rap

### One - U2
- Irish rock band
- Bono, The Edge, and the rest, Adam Clayton bass, Larry Mullen Jr
- Pop album, Pemon, Disctotech, much derided. Spinal Tap moment getting stuck in a 40 foot mirror lemon in Norway.
- Entering the 90s a stadium rock band.
- Dark, sexy, industrial, throwaway, trashy
- Pivotal album between desire to innovate (Bono and Edge) vs. stick with what works (rhythm section). One is the compromise.
- Achtung baby transitional album
- Need to watch Numb video and disco tech video

### Make Em Say Uhh - Master P (1997)
- Business savvy, better business than rapper
- Owns masters, 80:20 deal
- Created an empire

### One in a Million - Aaliyah
- First album produced by R Kelly (and wrote most songs) when she was 15, age ain’t but a number
- Blend of street and sweet
- R Kelly sex trafficking and racketeering, falsifying her age to marry her
- Missy Elliot / Timbaland collaborations
- Had to grow up too quickly
- Died in a plane crash at 2001, age of 21?
- Parents restricted access to music after her death for decades
- Are you that something, baby sample, Timbaland

### Would? - Alice In Chains
- Heaviest of Seattle’s early grunge bands
- Bands name was “Sleez”, then “Alice ‘n Chainz” (Guns ‘n Roses?)
- From the origins of hair metal, Motley Crue and poison and warrant and all that
- Suburban metal band
- Jerry and Lane, often harmonizing
- Lots of songs about heroin
- Lane died of a drug overdose in 2002
- Mad season, album of heroin rehab group
- Need to listen to Alice In Chains unplugged

### Enter Sandman - Metallica (1991)
- Black Album sold 16 million in US
- Alongside Slayer, Anthrax, and Megadeth as thrash metal
- Master or Puppets, One
- Tour bus crashed and killed Cliff Burton (bass player)
- Built to be more popular, pivot point between old and new
- Sold out to be more popular, make metal more popular

### Nothing Compares 2 U - Sinead O’Connor
- Originally a Prince song (recorded 1984)
- Had a terribly creepy interaction with Prince
- Shaved head after studio/record folks were pressuring her to have longer hair and be more feminine
- Understated music video with mainly just her head
- She quickly got sick of her #1 song
- Tore up Pope’s picture in 1992 as guest on SNL, protesting child abuse in Catholic Church
- Punk attitude

### Walking on the Sun - Smash Mouth (1997)
- Heavily returned record because the single and the rest of album are very different
- Unpredictable rock band, reinvent to be more poppy, similar to Red Hot Chili Peppers and Sugar Ray
- Guitar tone
- Leads to… “All Star”, Shrek, then later turned to memes
- “Shrek is a daily occurrence for us”

### No Rain - Blind Melon (1992)
- Bee girl video, hit single
- Died of a cocaine overdose on tour in 1995

### Black Hole Sun - Soundgarden (1994)
- Has a Strong Songs episode…
- Disturbing music video
- Wearing fork from Shannon from Blind Melon, and something from Andy Wood from Mother Love Bone, too many dead guys things
- Contrived shirtlessness
- Suicide in 2017
- First album in 1987
- Between metal band and punk band
- Rusty cage from 91 album
- Later collaboration with Rage to make Audioslave

### Macarena - Los Del Rio (1996)
- 1993 original
- Famous remixes: show me love, singer had flue, remix added keyboard, don’t get paid
- Tom’s diner, do do do in the fade out
- Miss you
- Bayside boys remix

### Smells Like Teen Spirit - Nirvana (1991)
- Story of living under the bridge
- Story of buying first guitar from mom’s boyfriend (uncle?) guns
- First concert not black Flag
- Aberdeen to Olympia
- Fist album 1988. Kurt left handed guitar. Nova Celtic bass.
- 1989 bleach.
- Married Courtney love in 1992 and had a daughter.
- Committed suicide in 1994.
- Smells like Nirvana, Weird Al.
- Punk rock attitude. Rejected fame but also wanted it.
- In Utero. 1992?
- Unplugged album released 6 months after suicide. Meat puppets.

### Yellow Ledbetter - Pearl Jam (1992)
- First album Ten. Second is Versus.
- Jeremy CD single had Yellow Ledbetter on it.
- Mudhoney and Mother Love Bone.
