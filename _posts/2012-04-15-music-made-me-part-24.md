<p><a href="zune://navigate/?trackID=F5AE1200-0100-11DB-89CA-0019B92A3933">Inner City Blues - Marvin Gaye</a></p>  <p>I don't know what inspired Jessica to start delving into a 1971 album all of the sudden in 2003, but she did. It was less of a discovery of Marvin Gaye and more of a rediscovery. &quot;What's Going On&quot; is a great album, but there was no question about what song to put in here. Makes me wanna holler...</p>  
<!--more-->

<p><a href="zune://navigate/?trackID=89BD2F00-0500-11DB-89CA-0019B92A3933">Love and Happiness - Al Green</a></p>  <p>At the same time as Jessica was rolling out the Marvin Gaye she was also dropping the Al Green. I think she did this specifically to confuse me. They sound very different to me now, but at the time I was very mixed up. Al Green seems to on average have a groovier sexier sound, but Marvin Gaye is the one to really call out &quot;Let's Get It On&quot; and lay down some &quot;Sexual Healing&quot;. However Marvin Gaye is the one with the thoughtful social commentary, while Al Green is the Reverend. It was a bit disorienting.</p>  <p>But ever since I'm prone to, as Morphine put it, &quot;put the Al Green on&quot;. You really can't go wrong. Everybody wins.</p>  

<p><a href="zune://navigate/?trackID=33671200-0100-11DB-89CA-0019B92A3933">I Believe In A Thing Called Love - The Darkness</a></p>  <p>Music games. They're a huge thing now (or maybe a huge thing that's jumped the shark a bit), but not so long ago they were new and niche.</p>  <p>I remember Ben first bringing around <em>Karaoke Revolution</em> to our gaming gatherings. His idea was simple: he wanted to get better at singing, and a game seemed like a great feedback mechanism to make that happen. The idea of a game judging your musical ability was still a novel idea back then. So we played this game, and soon it became clear that non-gamer people were interested in this too. Even, gasp, girls.</p>  <p>Fast forward to the sequel, and we have a growing crowd of people. But no one has the balls to tackle this song. Until Rand does. And he fucking <em>nails</em> it. He committed, he delivered, and it was awesome.</p>  <p>For years I was waiting for this song to make it to <em>Rock Band</em>. It always seemed like an obvious pick. Fun and challenging vocals, rocking guitars, and of course a big cup of awesome flair. I mean, have you seen <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRYNYb30nxU">the video</a>? One of the best <em>ever</em>. Anyway, someone had once figured out the rights to get this song into a music game, so what was the hold up? I did eventually get my wish, although only a year ago. I'm still waiting for someone to really step it up and deliver the vocals...</p>  

<p><a href="zune://navigate/?trackID=10FF7C06-0100-11DB-89CA-0019B92A3933">Habanera - Carmen (Donkey Konga)</a></p>  <p>Okay, so no orchestral recording of this song is going to accurately represent how this song exists in my head. Thankfully bored people on the internet exist to archive <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tCpkaxm0Bus">this stuff</a> for me.</p>  <p>So, somehow this crazy Gamecube game that came with a plastic bongo controller made it stateside. The track selection was… well… I'd be generous even calling it &quot;uneven&quot;. But beating drums to music is pretty much fun no matter what. Even if they're fake drums, and even if the music is often terrible.</p>  <p>By the time <em>Donkey Konga 2</em> came around we had a setup with a TV and two bongo controllers at work. When the mood was right at the end of the day we'd have some drinks, bang some bongos, and have an all awesome time.</p>  <p>Oddly enough, this song was the best one. So many times Jon and I would be scrolling through the track list, pondering, and just end up back on Habanera. It makes zero sense until you've actually drummed along to it.</p>  

<p><a href="zune://navigate/?trackID=93BB3700-0100-11DB-89CA-0019B92A3933">Go It Alone - Beck</a></p>  <p>Beck, you're not so sad anymore are you? You've had years to heal that broken heart, and as a result you were able to give birth to <em>Guero</em>. Which I like to believe is so much better for the heartbreak that preceded it. So... thanks for taking one for the team.</p>  <p>This song… yum. It's a foot stomping, snappin', can't help but bob your head kind of a groove-a-long. A minimalistic triumph. So. Good.</p>  <p>I actually first hit this album via the <em>GameBoy Variations</em> remix. Okay, it's not all proper chip tune mixes, but the thought is still there. I actually prefer the <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SAmvp0JUA_Y">remix</a> version of &quot;Hell Yes&quot; to the album version. The &quot;Girl&quot; <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NM094Q55yE">remix</a> is also pretty rad.</p>
