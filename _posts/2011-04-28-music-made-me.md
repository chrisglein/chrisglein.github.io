<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
</div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
In the<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>book High Fidelity (and the <a href="http://movies.netflix.com/WiMovie/High_Fidelity/60000166?trkid=2361637">movie</a> too, for that matter), the main character is obsessed with making "Top 5" lists and categorizing his music collection.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>There's a moment where he decides to organize his records <i>autobiographically </i>- the order in which he experienced them.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>I've always been intrigued by that idea: trying to express one's journey in life through the music that accompanied it.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>So I'm going to do just that, except instead of sorting stacks of records I'll be producing a playlist.</div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
<br /></div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
Unlike the character in the book I have no intention of organizing every single piece of music I own in such a matter.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Really I just want to capture the inflection points.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>The problem is that as soon as you introduce choice into this exercise it becomes an entirely different activity.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>In defining the timeline of my life, what music was most important?</div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
<br /></div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
Do I choose songs that represent the evolution of my taste in music?<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Or do I choose songs that are steeped in personal memory?<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Sometimes hearing a song can take me to a time, or to a specific feeling.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Some songs are tied so tightly to poignant memories that hearing them transports me to an exact moment.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Clearly a musical timeline of my life has to include these songs.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>They may not represent me musically, but they do represent my human experience.</div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
<br /></div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
As I've tried to put this list together it's become clear that I can't just do one or the other.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>A progression through my music tastes alone is soulless;<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp;&nbsp;</span>a trip through the<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>highlights of emotional moments lacks context.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>There needs to be an intentional blend in order to achieve a sensible storyline.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>It's a tricky task, and one I've been working on for months now.</div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
<br /></div>
<div style="font-family: Calibri; font-size: 11.0pt; margin: 0in;">
I've finished reconstructing my timeline, and now it's down to the logistics of writing, linking, and presenting this to the vast anonymity of the internet.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Let this post serve as an introduction to the project.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>The next time you hear about it we'll jump right into the early years of my exposure to music.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Everything from Bob Marley to Michael Jackson to even New Kids on the Block.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>You heard me,<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp;</span>I'm baring it all.<br />
