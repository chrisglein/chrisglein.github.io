Created by the same people (and sharing some of the same cast) as <a href="http://www.netflix.com/Movie/Shaun_of_the_Dead/70003227">Shaun of the Dead</a>, I was totally excited for <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0425112/">Hot Fuzz</a>, even though I didn't know anything else about it.  Which is dangerous, because coming into a movie with expectations of any kind is basically asking for trouble.  But Hot Fuzz totally delivered, and is definitely the best movie I've caught in the theater in a long time.
<!--more-->

Although Hot Fuzz covers different subject matter than Shaun of the Dead, they are very similar in that they succeed at both honoring and satirizing their respective genres.  Just as Shaun of the Dead made a good zombie film, Hot Fuzz is a good action film.  It makes good use of the same sort of rhythmic tension building transitions that Shaun had, and the action scenes are simultaneously awesome and hilarious.  The characters also get a good amount of development, and are very different characters from Shaun, even though the actors are the same.  It's a movie that manages to be many things, but the one thing that it never compromises on is being flat out fun.  Seriously, it was awesome - go see it.<br /><br />The movie was preceded by a trailer for <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0425413/">another</a> <a href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0670408/">Simon Pegg</a> movie.  And although the trailer pretty much told me nothing, I'm already excited to see more.
